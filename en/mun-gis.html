What we solve for municipalities

Three areas where aerial survey and GIS deliver a measurable result for a municipality — in budget, in land, and in the digital archive.

01 — Land fund

Land fund audit

Automatic detection of illegally extended fences, unauthorized structures and boundary violations.

  • Detecting land grabs and boundary overreach
  • Budget growth through fines and legalization
  • Reconciling actual boundaries with cadastral data
02 — Territory

Closing the land shortage

A high-precision point cloud reveals abandoned, unregistered or vacant zones suitable for re-planning.

  • Finding unregistered and ownerless plots
  • Precise measurement of vacant zones for new planning
  • A base for urban planning decisions
03 — Digital necropolis

Building a “Digital Necropolis”

Digitizing old paper registry books and linking them to an interactive digital map.

  • Converting archival registry books to digital form
  • Linking burial records to the interactive map
  • Search by full name for citizens and the diaspora abroad
